Every number here states what it does not say.

Most agency case studies are written to persuade. These were graded before they were written, and most of them did not survive.

39 client accounts assessed. 7 are publishable.

Each account was inspected file by file, then every positive verdict was adversarially refuted before it was allowed to stand. 15 had insufficient evidence. 4 may never be published at all. The rules every claim was tested against:

  1. A lead, call, form-fill, application or registration never carries a dollar value and never carries the word ROAS.
  2. Every claim needs a window. A number without a stated period is unpublishable.
  3. Peak is not blended. If a figure is the best month, say so in the same sentence.
  4. Modelled is not tracked. The word travels with the number.
  5. Nothing is named without written permission on file.
  6. Check always_use_default_value before believing any conversion value.
  7. Judge real-versus-proxy by WEIGHTED value contribution, not by whether flat actions exist.

What this number does not say
  • Booked revenue is channel-mixed across roughly ten sources. Most of it is not paid search, and none of it is credited to the ad account here.

  • No improvement is claimed. The eras differ in what each could measure as well as how each performed, and the data cannot separate the two.
  • The eras are not like-for-like: one is entirely Search, another roughly a quarter Performance Max.

Regional broadband ISP

What it actually costs to acquire a subscriber, against the industry benchmark.

$246 cost per acquired subscriber on one platform-market, against a $300-500 industry SAC benchmark

What this number does not say
  • This is modelled, not measured. Every conversion is a lead; no purchase event exists, so no revenue figure and no return can be stated.
  • The figure is one platform in one market. The same close rate gives materially higher costs in the other market and on social. Read the table, not the best number in it.
  • The close rate is the client's own, taken from their sales reporting. It is quoted, not estimated.
  • No lifetime value, payback or return is available: the client's reporting records no churn model.
  • Lead volume itself carries a caveat, because several form actions are counted at once.

Publishable as: cost per acquired customer against a cited benchmark. NEVER as ROAS.

Apparel · Shopify

A platform setting was adding a fixed $74 to every new-customer order, and a second was adding $74 again to lapsed customers. The account was reporting revenue it had not earned. Both were dropped to $0.01, and the fix is checkable in the client's own exports by differencing reported value against original value.

CA$63,146 spend to CA$385,651 tracked Shopify revenue, 6.11x over three clean contiguous months

What this number does not say
  • Three contiguous months, stated in Canadian dollars. The ratio is currency-neutral; the dollar totals are not.
  • The earlier month affected by value inflation is excluded rather than restated, so no platform-added value reaches this total.
  • No lift is claimed. The padded period is not a 'before' to improve on, because its numbers were not measuring the right thing.
  • The final month was captured on its closing day and is still settling.
